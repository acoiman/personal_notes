# Natural Hazards

**Summary**: Notes on floods, droughts, storms and other natural hazards, including event datasets and risk analysis.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054): An open global [[Data]] set of 2.6 million historical flood events drawn from news articles in more than 150 countries, published by Google researchers and collaborators (Zenodo, Feb 2026). It ships as a single Parquet file under a CC-BY-4.0 license. Relevant to [[Climate_Change]]. *Keywords*: floods, disaster events, news mining, global dataset, Parquet, hydrology
