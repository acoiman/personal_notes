# Agriculture

**Summary**: Notes on agricultural mapping, crop monitoring, and field-level analysis.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): A [[Python]] toolkit that delineates agricultural field boundaries from [[Remote_Sensing]] imagery (Sentinel-2, Landsat, HLS, NAIP) and from Earth [[Embeddings]] (Google Satellite Embedding, TESSERA). It runs Delineate-Anything, Fields of The World, and foundation models such as Prithvi, with optional SAM refinement and crop-mask filtering. *Keywords*: field boundaries, segmentation, Sentinel-2, foundation models, SAM, Earth Engine
